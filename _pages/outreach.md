# Astronomy for everyone

The Universe belongs to curious people, not only to people with telescope time. I write, translate, teach, and speak about astronomy because a good question can begin anywhere — in a classroom, a bookshop, or a conversation beneath the night sky.

## Writing and translation

I have published more than 50 astronomy-related articles and translated five books, according to my October 2026 CV. My earlier website lists work with Chinese publications including *National Astronomy of China*, *Guangming Daily*, *Science World*, *Guokr*, and others. It also describes my Chinese translations of *Is Einstein Still Right?*, *Supernova*, and *Loops*. Publication and translation details can change; please contact me for a current bibliography of outreach writing.

## Teaching the sky

In 2025 I chaired the organising committee and lectured at the first **Edinburgh School of Extragalactic Astronomy**: five days of lectures organised by early-career researchers for about 120 participants. I taught sessions on distant-galaxy emission, radiation transfer, telescope proposals, and reading scientific papers. I have also supervised summer research students and taught practical observing and data reduction, including in Kenya.

## Public talks and conversations

My outreach talks range from the hidden history of star formation to what it is actually like to work as an astronomer. I have given invited public talks in English and Chinese and joined podcasts to discuss astronomy with wider audiences. The common thread is to begin with a familiar question — *What can light tell us?* — before introducing the methods that make an answer possible.

## Art, access, and community

My earlier site records work on the astronomy-themed art exhibition **Cosmic Elements (步天歌)**, as well as educational activities for students in regions with fewer science resources. I am interested in collaborations that make astronomy accessible across languages and disciplines.

**中文简介：**我希望把天文学讲成每个人都能参与的探索。无论是写作、翻译、课堂还是观测活动，最好的起点通常不是术语，而是一个真诚的问题：我们怎样知道遥远宇宙里正在发生什么？
