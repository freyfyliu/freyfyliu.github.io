## Selected collaborations

These are a few papers in which I contributed as a co-author. The [ADS record](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/search/q=orcid%3A0000-0003-1386-3676&sort=date%20desc%2C%20bibcode%20desc&p_=0) gives the full author lists and publication details.

- [*Strength in Numbers: Red Galaxies Bolster the Cosmic Star Formation Rate Density at z > 3*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05740), Barrufet et al. (2026). Red galaxies help reveal star formation hidden in ultraviolet surveys.
- [*The JWST EXCELS survey: Outflows in 1.5 < z < 5 quiescent galaxies are likely relics from episodic AGN activity*](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/search/q=title%3A%22The%20JWST%20EXCELS%20survey%3A%20Outflows%22), Taylor et al. (2026). A view of how black-hole activity may leave fingerprints in galaxies that have largely stopped forming stars.
- [*JWST PRIMER: A new multi-field determination of the evolving galaxy UV luminosity function at redshifts z ~ 9-15*](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024MNRAS.533.3222D/abstract), Donnan et al. (2024). A census of very early galaxies using JWST imaging.
